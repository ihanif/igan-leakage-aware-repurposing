## GWAS locus context

TGFB1 encodes TGF-beta1, the master fibrosis regulator and a direct Kiryluk 2023 GWAS locus (rank 34 of 3,005, score 0.428). In IgAN, TGF-beta1 drives mesangial cell activation, endothelial-to-mesenchymal transition (EndMT), and ECM deposition leading to irreversible nephron loss. No approved IgAN therapy reverses established fibrosis. TGF-beta inhibitors are in Phase 2/3 for liver fibrosis and IPF but have not entered IgAN-specific trials.
